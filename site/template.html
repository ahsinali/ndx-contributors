How contributions are worked out

Each stock's points in the index today are its index weight times today's close. Rolling that back by the stock's own price change gives its points at the start of the period, and the difference is its contribution. Summed across members, the contributions add to the index's move when nothing was rebalanced in between.

Stocks that joined during the period count from the close before they joined, so their earlier gains aren't credited. Parent stocks are adjusted for spin-offs (Comcast/Versant, Honeywell/Solstice and Honeywell Aerospace). Prices are closing prices, not including dividends.

From the go-live date on, each day's contribution is logged with the weights in force that day (start-of-day weight × day's return × prior index close), so periods chain through rebalances and membership changes. Days before that are estimated by rolling the starting weights back with each stock's own price, which can't see rebalances; that is what the Other line picks up on long windows.

Weights start from the Invesco QQQ holdings (the fund that tracks this index) as of — and roll forward daily with prices. When the index changes membership or rebalances, the daily check flags it and the weights are refreshed.

Data

Closing prices and index levels: Yahoo Finance. Starting weights: Invesco QQQ holdings; SpaceX, Moderna and Copart estimated. Membership changes: Nasdaq announcements.
